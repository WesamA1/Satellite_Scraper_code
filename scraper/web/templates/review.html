Review

Keep, reject, or mark images as duplicates one at a time, and label which specific satellite variant each one shows. Rejects and duplicates move to quarantine, same as the Filter stage. Duplicate scan

Finds byte-for-byte identical files: copies within the same family are removed automatically (the best copy is kept, the rest go to quarantine), and copies under different families are left for you to pick the family. Then groups look-alikes (same or similar image, different file) for you to decide on below.
