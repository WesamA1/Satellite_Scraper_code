Check set

A fixed random sample of images with a person's answers, used to score image-screening models fairly (OWLv2 now, a larger vision-language model later). Two questions per image: how many separate satellites are in it, and, when there's one, whether you'd recognize it. Partial views are fine. "Not recognizable" means half-built, too small or too blurry to tell. Saved in data/check_sets/screening.csv, so it travels with the data folder. Back to Train model.
